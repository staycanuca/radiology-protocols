    11/23/23
    MRI Long Bones
    Reviewed
    06/06/26
    Use this protocol for non tumor/infection indications involving the humerus, forearm, femur or tibia/fibula.
    If the indication is for a focal concern, use a small field-of-view (FOV) centered on the area of interest.
    Otherwise use a field-of-view (FOV) centered on the long bone with coverage as follows:
    include the joints on both ends of the bone (top/bottom coverage)
    include anterior and posterior soft tissues (front/back coverage)
    include lateral and medial soft tissues (right/left coverage)
    Go to MRIMaster.com for a guide of proper positioning.
